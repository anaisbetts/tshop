# Sample art

Placeholder tile icons for the theme gallery (`/gallery`), copied from
`design/src/tshop/art/icons/`. Credits and caveats are in
`design/src/tshop/art/SOURCES.md`. Not a shipped catalog.

`assets/fonts/` holds the Latin subset of **M PLUS Rounded 1c** (SIL Open Font
License, `OFL.txt`), converted to TTF from `@fontsource/m-plus-rounded-1c`.
